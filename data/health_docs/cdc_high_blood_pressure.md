# CDC — High Blood Pressure Risk Factors
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/high-blood-pressure/risk-factors/
Accessed: 2026-10-06

Risk factors for high blood pressure include physical inactivity, unhealthy diet, some medical conditions such as diabetes, too much alcohol and tobacco use. Family history and age also influence risk. Risk factors can be grouped into health conditions, lifestyle and family history. Controllable risk factors can sometimes be reduced through healthier choices and appropriate management.
