# WHO — Physical Activity
Source: https://www.who.int/initiatives/behealthy/physical-activity/
Accessed: 2026-10-06

For adults, WHO recommends at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity per week, or 75 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity, or an equivalent combination. For additional benefits, adults can increase moderate-intensity activity to 300 minutes per week or an equivalent amount. Muscle-strengthening activities should involve major muscle groups on two or more days per week.
