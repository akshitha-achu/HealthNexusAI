# WHO — Healthy Diet
Source: https://www.who.int/en/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet
Accessed: 2026-10-06

A healthy diet helps protect against malnutrition and noncommunicable diseases including diabetes, heart disease and stroke. WHO describes adequacy, balance, moderation and diversity as core principles. Diets emphasizing minimally processed or unprocessed foods, whole grains, vegetables, fruits, pulses and other nutritious foods are encouraged, while foods high in unhealthy fats, free sugars and sodium should be limited. For adults, WHO recommends limiting salt to less than 5 grams per day and aiming for at least 400 grams of fruits and vegetables per day.
