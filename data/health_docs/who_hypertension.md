# WHO — Hypertension
Source: https://www.who.int/health-topics/hypertension
Accessed: 2026-10-06

Hypertension is persistently raised blood pressure. It can increase the risk of heart, brain, kidney and other diseases. Many people do not notice symptoms, so measurement of blood pressure is important for detection. Modifiable risk factors include unhealthy diets, excessive salt intake, physical inactivity, tobacco use, harmful alcohol use and overweight or obesity. Family history, older age and conditions such as diabetes or kidney disease are non-modifiable or associated risk factors. Management includes reducing modifiable risks, checking blood pressure regularly and consulting health professionals.
