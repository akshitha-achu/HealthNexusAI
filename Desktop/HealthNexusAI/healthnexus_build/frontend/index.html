Health Assessment

Five professional questions. Clinical details appear only when you say you have a report.

Guided assessment

01 · Cardiovascular & Medical History

Have you ever been diagnosed with or treated for a cardiovascular or metabolic condition?

02 · Family & Genetic Risk

Is there a history of cardiovascular disease, diabetes, hypertension, or high cholesterol in your immediate family?

03 · Lifestyle & Physiological Profile

How would you describe your typical lifestyle and physical activity pattern?

04 · Current Cardiovascular Symptoms

During normal activity or physical exertion, have you experienced any symptoms that concern you?

05 · Clinical Data Availability

Do you have a recent medical examination or diagnostic report containing cardiovascular measurements?

Clinical report details
Enter only information available in your report. Do not estimate or guess. If a value is missing, leave it blank.
For this demonstration, the model requires age and sex. Clinical fields are optional at the user interface level and are imputed by the preprocessing pipeline when unavailable. Coverage is shown with the result so the user can see how much clinical information was actually supplied.

Safety & Transparency

Designed to make the boundaries of the prototype clear.

Educational, not diagnostic

The risk model is a machine-learning estimate. It is not a clinical diagnosis or a substitute for professional care.

Evidence before generation

Health AI retrieves relevant chunks from curated public-health documents before generating an answer. If evidence is insufficient, it says so.

No guessing clinical values

Clinical measurements are shown only after the user confirms that a medical report is available. Users are told to leave unavailable values blank.

Data minimization

The local prototype stores prediction requests and results in SQLite for the assignment. Do not enter real personal health records.
