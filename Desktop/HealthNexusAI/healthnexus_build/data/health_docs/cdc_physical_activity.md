# CDC — Health Benefits of Physical Activity for Adults
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/health-benefits/adults.html
Accessed: 2026-10-06

Regular physical activity provides important health benefits for chronic disease prevention. It can help improve sleep and reduce short-term anxiety. Regular activity can lower the risk of heart disease, stroke and type 2 diabetes, and can help reduce the risk of weight gain. Physical activity is also relevant to blood pressure and overall cardiovascular health.
