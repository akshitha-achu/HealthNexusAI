# CDC — Heart Disease Risk Factors
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/heart-disease/risk-factors/
Accessed: 2026-10-06

Health conditions, lifestyle, age and family history can increase the risk for heart disease. Key risk factors include high blood pressure, high cholesterol and smoking. Some risk factors cannot be controlled, such as age and family history, while other factors can be changed through healthier behaviors and management of health conditions.
